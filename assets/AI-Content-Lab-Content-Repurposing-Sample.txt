AI CONTENT LAB
CONTENT REPURPOSING — SAMPLE

How one piece of useful content can become multiple social-media assets.

==================================================
SOURCE CONTENT
==================================================

Original topic:
"5 Things to Check Before Buying a Home"

Original format:
6-slide educational carousel

==================================================
REPURPOSING PLAN
==================================================

1. CAROUSEL
Use the original 6-slide carousel.
Purpose: Education and saves.

2. SHORT-FORM REEL
Turn the five points into a 20–30 second text-based Reel.
Hook:
"Buying a home? Check these 5 things first."

Purpose: Reach and discovery.

3. STORY SERIES
Turn each point into one Story frame.
Frame 1: Location
Frame 2: Property condition
Frame 3: Neighborhood
Frame 4: Total costs
Frame 5: Future value
Final frame CTA:
"Have a property you're considering? Send us a message."

Purpose: Engagement and replies.

4. SINGLE-IMAGE POST
Create a simple checklist:
"Before You Buy: 5 Things to Check"

Purpose: Quick-reference content.

5. CAPTION
Use the original topic to create a longer educational caption
that explains why each point matters.

CTA:
"Save this checklist for your next property viewing."

Purpose: Education and saves.

6. FOLLOW-UP FAQ
Create a second post from the most common question generated
by the original content.

Example:
"What costs should buyers plan for beyond the purchase price?"

Purpose: Continue the conversation.

==================================================
THE REPURPOSING SYSTEM
==================================================

1 original idea
↓
1 carousel
↓
1 Reel
↓
1 Story sequence
↓
1 single-image post
↓
1 caption
↓
1 follow-up FAQ

Result:
One useful topic becomes a small content series instead of being
used only once.

==================================================
DENTAL EXAMPLE
==================================================

Source:
"5 Signs You Shouldn't Ignore a Toothache"

Repurpose into:
• Educational carousel
• Short-form Reel: "5 toothache warning signs"
• Story quiz: "Which symptom should you get checked?"
• Single-image checklist
• Educational caption
• Follow-up FAQ: "When should tooth pain be evaluated?"

WHAT AI CONTENT LAB PROVIDES
==================================================

• Identify reusable ideas from existing content

• Create new hooks and CTAs
• Turn long-form information into short-form content
• Create follow-up content from audience questions
